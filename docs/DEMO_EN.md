# Three-minute demo script

Use actual recordings and results you have permission to show. Prepare different recordings of one specific phenomenon, a lookalike negative, and distinct posting accounts. Do not use synthetic fixtures as evidence of real-world accuracy.

| Time | On screen | Spoken line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Two differently recorded clips of the same precise phenomenon | “These videos look different. They are part of the same recurring phenomenon. In a feed, that repetition can be hard to notice.” |
| 0:20–0:45 | Popup: N = 3. First and second accounts observed | “ContextLens counts the distinct accounts I actually encounter in this browser. It recognizes a specific phenomenon across formats, such as a tutorial and a performance.” |
| 0:45–1:05 | Third distinct account pauses and blurs. A lookalike negative stays visible | “At my chosen limit, the next matching clip pauses. A similar-looking but different subject stays visible. The broad category is never the grouping key.” |
| 1:05–1:25 | Click Show video, then demonstrate the investigation action on another blurred clip | “I can show it immediately. Or I can open OriginTrace to investigate sources and context. Search results are leads, not proof of the original upload.” |
| 1:25–2:00 | Actual Why grouped? evidence; architecture or recorded request stages | “Meta Muse Spark first describes the clip without seeing candidates. We search our stored observations, then independently verify the precise identity. A shared soundtrack, label or celebrity cannot bypass that check. Uncertain clips stay visible.” |
| 2:00–2:35 | A real executed baseline/improved comparison with negatives and denominators | “We compared the old and new inference paths on the same labelled clips, including different recordings and lookalike negatives. Here are the measured precision, recall, false blurs, latency and model attempts.” |
| 2:35–3:00 | Return to the feed; viewer reveals a clip | “This prototype is limited to observed accounts and accessible frames. It does not know the whole platform, verify the first uploader or reliably identify audio. ContextLens gives people a small, concrete choice: when have I seen enough? Fewer repeats. More context. Your choice.” |

If the real benchmark is not completed, replace the 2:00–2:35 segment with: “The current validation covers software regressions and controlled synthetic API checks. We have not yet measured real Instagram accuracy.” Show the validation scope instead of invented scores. This is honest, but a measured real-data comparison would provide stronger judging evidence.

Record a continuous interaction take for the counting, blur, reveal and investigation actions. Edit it into the three-minute video without making a prerecorded or synthetic response appear to be a live model result. Keep keys and pairing tokens out of the recording. Confirm current official upload requirements before submitting.
