TRANSPARENCY / 003

How OriginTrace works.

An evidence-led workflow, with uncertainty shown explicitly.

01

Observe

Sample video frames; extract identifying objects, text, locations and potential search phrases.

02

Retrieve

Search external sources. The earliest indexed, date-stamped result is not necessarily the original upload.

03

Weigh

Apply transparent heuristics: website significance (50%), chronology (30%) and textual relevance (20%).

04

Disclose

List links, dates, limitations and uncertainty. Scores do not establish authenticity or truth.

Text matching identifies related reporting; it does not prove that an article depicts the same clip. Conflicting publication dates are excluded from earliest-date selection. Search dates may be unavailable; publication date is not upload date. No claim should be called verified without independent corroboration.
